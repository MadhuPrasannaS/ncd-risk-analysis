India-specific warning: SDG 3.4 & WHO 25×25 targets at risk
ICMR-NCDIR projects India's premature NCD mortality will decline only 13.9% by 2025 vs. the WHO target of 25%. For SDG 2030, a projected 15.6% decline vs. the required one-third reduction. India is projected to lose $4.58 trillion by 2030 from NCDs and mental health. Air pollution causes over 2 million deaths per year in India — one of the top three NCD drivers alongside tobacco and dietary risks.

Global critical concern: SDG 3.4 target at risk
The WHO predicts NCDs will account for 77% of all global disease burden by 2030. Projections estimate 75.5 million deaths and 2.44 billion DALYs by 2050. Urgent policy action is required on metabolic risks — particularly hypertension, obesity, and blood glucose control across all nations.
